## ✨ Smart Manager Lite – fast, efficient store management (Free)

**Edit WooCommerce products and variations**

Edit titles, SKUs, descriptions, categories, tags, attributes, tax class and tax status right in the grid. Turn on "Show Variations" and edit variations as easily as simple products. Add new products straight from the spreadsheet, or duplicate selected products, filtered products or your whole catalog in one click.

**Edit prices and sale prices**

Update regular and sale prices directly in the spreadsheet, with every product and variation in view, so you can spot and fix pricing mistakes at a glance.

**Stock manager and inventory**

Update stock quantity, stock status, manage stock and backorders in the grid. See a stock log for each product to know how fast stock moves, and sync stock log data from the Stock Manager for WooCommerce plugin. Export your stock columns to CSV whenever you need them.

**Edit attributes, categories, tags and SKUs**

Add or remove attributes, categories and tags, and fix SKUs, without leaving the spreadsheet.

**Edit WooCommerce orders**

Change order status, billing and shipping details, shipping method and currency from one screen. Customers get the usual WooCommerce emails when an order status changes. HPOS compatible.

**Edit WooCommerce coupons**

Update coupon amounts, types, usage limits and expiry dates, and create new coupons from the grid.

**Edit custom fields and ACF**

Smart Manager detects custom fields added by other plugins automatically, including ACF, Pods and Meta Box, so you can edit them next to your standard fields.

**Edit WordPress posts and pages**

Change titles, images and post status (draft to publish) for posts and pages in the same spreadsheet.

**Find exactly what to edit**

* Simple search across all columns
* Advanced Search with conditions like contains, is, greater than and less than
* Search, date and column filters
* Show, hide and sort columns, and reset to the default view

**Work faster**

* Import products from CSV
* Add, duplicate, trash and delete records
* Sticky headers, image previews and a distraction-free mode
* Works with 50+ popular WooCommerce and WordPress plugins

== Privacy & Usage Tracking ==

Smart Manager respects your privacy. Usage tracking is completely optional and turned **off by default**. No data is sent unless you explicitly opt in via the in-plugin notice or settings.

When enabled, we only collect 100% anonymous usage patterns (such as feature clicks, plugin version, and general store size) to help guide our feature roadmap. We **never** collect store sales, customer details, product data, or personal info.

Usage data is sent to [PostHog](https://posthog.com), our analytics provider, only after you opt in.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= How do I bulk edit products in WooCommerce? =

With Smart Manager Pro: open the Products dashboard, filter or select products, click **Bulk Edit**, choose a field and action (for example, increase regular price by 10%) and apply or schedule it. With the free version, you edit products directly in the spreadsheet.

= What's included in the free version? =

The full spreadsheet editor for products, variations, orders, coupons, posts and custom fields, with inline editing (up to 3 edits per save), Advanced Search, filters, CSV product import, stock column export, a stock log, and add, duplicate and delete. Bulk edit and batch update are part of Smart Manager Pro. [See the full comparison →](https://www.storeapps.org/docs/sm-lite-vs-pro/?utm_source=wprepo&utm_medium=web&utm_campaign=smwp_readme)

= How do I bulk edit prices and sale prices? =

In Smart Manager Pro, filter the products (by category, attribute, stock status or any field), choose Bulk Edit, then increase or decrease the regular or sale price by an amount or percentage. In the free version you update prices directly in the grid.

= Can I manage stock and inventory? =

Yes. Edit stock quantity, stock status, manage stock and backorders in the grid, with a stock log per product. Pro adds bulk stock updates across many products and variations at once.

= Can I edit product variations? =

Yes. Turn on "Show Variations" to edit variations in the grid. Pro adds bulk editing of every variation field and copying values between variations.

= Can I bulk edit attributes, categories, tags and SKUs? =

You can edit them in the grid for free. Bulk editing them across many products at once is part of Pro.

= Can I bulk edit WooCommerce orders? =

You can edit order status and details in the grid for free. Pro adds bulk editing of orders and line items.

= Can I bulk edit coupons? =

You can edit coupon amounts, limits and expiry dates in the grid for free. Pro adds bulk editing across all coupons.

= Can I bulk edit WooCommerce Subscriptions? =

Yes, with Smart Manager Pro.

= Can I edit ACF and other custom fields? =

Yes. Smart Manager detects custom fields automatically, including ACF, Pods and Meta Box, and you can edit them in the grid. Pro adds bulk editing.

= What is batch update? =

Another name for bulk edit: one action that changes many records at once, such as raising prices by 10% for a category. It's part of Smart Manager Pro.

= Can I undo changes? =

Yes, with Smart Manager Pro, from Edit History. Keep a backup before large changes on a live store.

= Do I still need CSV import/export? =

Mostly no. You edit directly in the grid. Product CSV import and stock column export are free; Pro exports all or filtered records.

= Is it compatible with HPOS? =

Yes. Smart Manager works with WooCommerce High-Performance Order Storage.

= Is Smart Manager GDPR-ready? =

Yes. Smart Manager is GDPR-ready. Usage tracking is optional and turned off by default, and no data is sent unless you opt in. See the Privacy & Usage Tracking section for details.

= Where do I report security bugs? =

Through the [Patchstack Vulnerability Disclosure Program](https://patchstack.com/database/vdp/9e5fb391-884c-41c9-8f84-e0e334baa3d4).
